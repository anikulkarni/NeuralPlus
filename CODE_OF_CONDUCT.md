# Code of conduct

NeuralPlus contributors are expected to communicate professionally and respectfully.

Constructive technical disagreement is welcome. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community and project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Reports will be handled with discretion and without retaliation.
